The foundation

Your files in the middle. Apps around them.

Epicenter is a foundation for building personal apps over Markdown and SQLite files you own. Apps give you interfaces for capturing, editing, and organizing that data.

  1. Files you can open without us.

    Read your Markdown in a text editor, open attachments with ordinary tools, and query a generated SQLite index. The saved files stay accessible outside the app.

  2. Apps that stand on their own.

    Apps can have their own repositories, releases, and domains. Use one, change it, or keep the files without it.

  3. Room for the one you make.

    Build another interface over a format you understand. Compatible apps can work with the same files.
